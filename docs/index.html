IQRA: a free Quran app for Windows and Android

IQRA (اقرأ, "Read") is a free, open-source Quran app with the Madani mushaf, recitation audio, translations, tafsir, hadith, prayer times, qibla and adhan. No ads, no account, and it works offline.

What you get

Read the Quran

The 604-page Madani mushaf laid out like the printed book, plus a verse-by-verse view with word-by-word meanings, transliteration and tafsir.

Listen

Recitation from 21 reciters, a synced Now Playing view, speed, repeat and sleep timer, with lock-screen controls on Android.

Translations and tafsir

62 Quran translations in 40+ languages and six tafsirs.

Prayer times and adhan

Prayer times for 240+ cities or your location, 14 calculation methods, adhan and reminders, and a live qibla compass.

Hadith and stories

168 hadith with explanations, the Forty Hadith of an-Nawawi, 211 stories, duas and Hisn al-Muslim.

Private and free

Open source (MIT), no ads, no account, no tracking. Your notes, bookmarks and progress stay on your device.

Questions

Is IQRA free?

Yes. IQRA is free and open source under the MIT License, with no ads, no account and no in-app purchases.

Does IQRA work offline?

Yes. The Qur'an text, stories, hadith and tools work offline. Only recitation audio and extra translations are downloaded the first time you use them.

Which Quran app is best for a Windows PC?

IQRA runs on Windows 10 and 11 with the 604-page Madani mushaf, a two-page spread on wide screens, keyboard shortcuts, translations, tafsir and recitation from 21 reciters.

Does IQRA have prayer times and adhan?

Yes. Prayer times for 240+ cities or your location with 14 calculation methods, adhan and reminders on Android, and a live qibla compass.
